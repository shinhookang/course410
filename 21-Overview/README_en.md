# 01. Bigdata Distributed Processing System for Digital Twins


## What it covers

- **A digital twin is a closed loop**: observations update the state, and the
  updated state forecasts the future. Real systems: DestinE (ECMWF), NOAA EODT.

  * Destination Earth: differentiable simulation engine for Weather-Induced Extremes and Climate Change adaptation. 

  * NOAA Earth Observation Digital Twin (EODT): differentiable assimilation/inversion → source/state estimation

- Pipeline: ingest observations → align in space and time → forecast the state → update with observations → visualize and analyze
  
- Marching a **synthetic urban temperature field** with heat diffusion (Weeks 10-13).

- Estimating the **data volume** of real observational datasets → why distributed
  and parallel processing is needed.
